Subject: Veterinary Medicine Withdrawal Periods Reference Guide

Medicine: Amoxicillin
Type: Antibiotic
Milk Withdrawal: 96 hours (4 days)

Medicine: Oxytetracycline
Type: Antibiotic
Milk Withdrawal: 7 days

Medicine: Meloxicam
Type: Anti-inflammatory (NSAID)
Milk Withdrawal: 5 days

Medicine: Ivermectin
Type: Antiparasitic
Milk Withdrawal: Do not use in lactating dairy cows.

Medicine: Enrofloxacin
Type: Antibiotic
Milk Withdrawal: Not approved for lactating dairy cattle.

Medicine: Penicillin G Procaine
Type: Antibiotic
Milk Withdrawal: 48 hours (2 days)

Medicine: Ceftiofur
Type: Antibiotic
Milk Withdrawal: 0 hours (No withdrawal)

Medicine: Dolo 650 (Paracetamol)
Type: Analgesic / Antipyretic
Milk Withdrawal: 24 hours (1 day)

Medicine: Salmol
Type: Bronchodilator / Respiratory Aid
Milk Withdrawal: 48 hours (2 days)
